**Goal:** Restore `muse serve` compatibility with Muse CLI 1.4.12 and record a durable follow-up for schema evolution across frequent CLI releases.

**Architecture:** Update the pinned `@muse-code/sdk` to 1.4.12, whose expected stable fingerprint matches the installed CLI. Preserve strict handshake validation. **Tech Stack:** TypeScript ESM, npm lockfile, Bun tests, Muse CLI/MSP SDK.

**Spec:** `docs/design.md` v1.3; `docs/plans/2026-10-21-opencode-muse.md` Tasks 1, 6, 8, 10, and 15; `AGENTS.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Communicate with Meta only through the official Muse CLI and `@muse-code/sdk` over `muse serve` stdio.
- Never read, copy, cache, log, or transmit Muse credentials or `~/.config/muse` contents.
- Keep exact served-fingerprint validation until a separately reviewed compatibility policy proves a safe alternative.
- Do not claim 1.4.12 support for protocol routes that are not rerun.
- Keep OpenChamber source unchanged.

## Review Focus

- A package or lockfile drift must not leave the SDK fingerprint at 1.4.11 while package metadata says 1.4.12.
- Schema change must not silently weaken the fail-closed handshake.
- Runtime checks must distinguish catalog discovery from actual inference and must not log prompts or generated prose.
- The compatibility issue must propose policy work, not claim additive changes are automatically safe.
